# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Nikki (the "App") is a journaling app provided by bannzai (the "Developer"). The App is built around the principle that no one else can read your journal: it is designed so that the Developer cannot collect or access your personal information or journal content. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

## 1. Information the Developer Collects

The Developer does not collect any personal information. The App contains no analytics tools, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking technologies. The Developer operates no servers.

## 2. Where Your Journal Data Is Stored

Your journal entries, templates, and settings are stored only on your device and in the private database of your own iCloud account (iCloud private database). Only your Apple Account can access this database; no third party, including the Developer, can access it. Data stored in iCloud is handled in accordance with [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 3. Face ID / Touch ID

The App uses biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) or your passcode, provided by the operating system, to unlock the App. Authentication is performed entirely within the OS; your biometric data is never shared with the App or the Developer.

## 4. Purchase Information

Purchases in the App are made through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Payments are processed by Apple, and the Developer never receives your payment details such as credit card numbers.

## 5. Statistics Provided by Apple

If you have opted in to sharing data with Apple, Apple may provide the Developer with crash reports and usage statistics in a form that cannot identify you. The collection of this information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

## 6. Sharing with Third Parties

Since the Developer holds no personal information about you, there is nothing to share with third parties.

## 7. GDPR / CCPA

Because the Developer does not collect or process your personal data, no processing subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) takes place on the Developer's side. Your journal data exists only in your own iCloud account and is governed by your agreement with Apple.

## 8. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your journal data at any time by deleting entries in the App, or by removing the App's data from your device's iCloud settings. You do not need to ask the Developer to delete your data — the Developer holds no data and therefore cannot delete it on your behalf.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from any user, including children.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page. Significant changes will be announced in the App or in the App Store release notes.
