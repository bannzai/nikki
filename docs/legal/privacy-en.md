# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Nikki (the "App") is a journaling app provided by bannzai (the "Developer"). The App is built around the principle that no one else can read your journal: it is designed so that the Developer cannot collect or access your personal information or journal content. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

## 1. Information the Developer Collects

The Developer does not collect any personal information. The App contains no analytics tools, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking technologies. The Developer operates no servers. For information sent to an external service to manage purchases, see Section 4.

## 2. Where Your Journal Data Is Stored

Your journal entries, templates, and settings are stored only on your device and in the private database of your own iCloud account (iCloud private database). Only your Apple Account can access this database; no third party, including the Developer, can access it. Data stored in iCloud is handled in accordance with [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 3. Face ID / Touch ID

The App uses biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID) or your passcode, provided by the operating system, to unlock the App. Authentication is performed entirely within the OS; your biometric data is never shared with the App or the Developer.

## 4. Purchase Information

Purchases in the App are made through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Payments are processed by Apple, and the Developer never receives your payment details such as credit card numbers.

To manage the status of the paid plan (Nikki Plus), the App uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. The following information, required to manage purchases and subscription status, is sent to RevenueCat:

- A random anonymous ID generated by the App (not linked to your name, email address, or other identity)
- App Store purchase and receipt information (purchased products, purchase dates, subscription status, etc.)
- Basic environment information such as OS version, App version, and region/language settings

This information is used solely to determine whether the paid plan is active and to restore purchases. Your journal content is never sent to RevenueCat. Data handled by RevenueCat is governed by [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## 5. Statistics Provided by Apple

If you have opted in to sharing data with Apple, Apple may provide the Developer with crash reports and usage statistics in a form that cannot identify you. The collection of this information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

## 6. Sharing with Third Parties

Except for the transmission to RevenueCat for purchase management described in Section 4, the Developer does not share your information with third parties. Your journal content is never shared with anyone.

## 7. GDPR / CCPA

Your journal data exists only in your own iCloud account and is governed by your agreement with Apple. The only information processed for purchase management is the anonymous ID and purchase information described in Section 4. If you wish to have this information deleted, please contact us via Section 11 and the Developer will request deletion from RevenueCat.

## 8. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your journal data at any time by removing the App's data from your device's iCloud settings (Settings &gt; Apple Account &gt; iCloud). This removes the data stored in your iCloud account. Note that simply deleting the App from your device does not remove the data already synced to iCloud. You do not need to ask the Developer to delete your data — the Developer holds no data and therefore cannot delete it on your behalf.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from any user, including children.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page. Significant changes will be announced in the App or in the App Store release notes.
